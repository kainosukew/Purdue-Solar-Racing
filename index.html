Front Suspension Dynamics

The core of our vehicle's handling characteristics lies within a custom-engineered double-wishbone architecture. The primary objective for this system was the absolute minimization of unsprung mass, all while strictly adhering to camber and toe compliance targets under maximum cornering thresholds.

Constructed from thin-wall chromoly tubing and paired with precision dampeners, the vehicle maintains an incredibly responsive ride profile. The kinematic geometry underwent rigorous iteration cycles to ensure the tire contact patch remains optimal regardless of track undulations.

Double A-Arm

Optimized roll center migration and camber gain during severe cornering loads.

Ackerman Steering

Custom machined steering rack mounts to eliminate scrub radius drag during turns.

Uprights & Siemens NX

Bridging the gap between theory and reality.

The front and rear uprights act as the vital junction for the wheels, suspension arms, and brake systems. Machined from aerospace-grade 7075-T6 aluminum, these components were subject to intense topological optimization protocols.

Leveraging the power of Siemens NX and NX Nastran, our mechanical team executed complex Finite Element Analysis (FEA). We simultaneously simulated 3G bump, 1.5G braking, and 2G cornering loads. NX's robust CAE environment allowed us to iteratively strip away material where stress concentrations were lowest, shedding crucial grams while maintaining a strict 1.4+ factor of safety.
